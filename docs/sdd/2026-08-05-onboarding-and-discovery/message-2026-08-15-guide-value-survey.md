# Message: the guide-value survey

From the `2026-08-04-next-steps` saga lead, 2026-08-15, carrying an operator ruling recorded dated
in the saga's `target-state.md` (the guide-value rubric). Your effort owns guide content, so the
survey is yours.

## The rubric

Guide content earns its place only by conveying concepts or higher-level synthesis not available
from a single CLI command. A block whose content is one command's output wearing guide clothes is
removed, and the guide points at the command instead, signpost-style all the way down. The operator:
"the guide's value is its ability to convey concepts and expose higher-level synthesis that isn't
readily available in a single cli command."

Kept by the rubric: `concept-` topics, the onboarding sequence and current-adoption assessment, the
agent contract, and action records with their consent boundaries and refusal alternatives. Cut
candidates: live instance lists (`resource list`), schema and field teaching (`resource explain`
after the grammar rename), relationship blocks (`graph show`, once it exists), and anything else a
single command already answers.

## What the survey is

Read-only. One inventory classifying every guide block keep/cut under the rubric, one row per block
(a topic mixing both gets multiple rows), each cut row naming the command that answers it, and the
machinery consequences priced: which renderers, block variants, projection paths, and JSON adapters
lose their last consumer if the cut rows go. The simplification pass's `findings.md` G4 and G5 are
prior evidence that the machinery prize may be large; the survey is what turns that from a guess
into a number.

Constraints:

- No removals ride the survey. It is reviewed first, like the sweep inventory was, by the saga lead
  and the complexity-critic lane.
- A block duplicating a command that does not exist yet (the relationship view, until the grammar
  child's `graph show` lands) is classified cut-later with its owner named, never cut-now.
- If the survey finds the cuttable surface small, say so plainly: keeping it is an acceptable
  answer, and the operator has said so.
- Machinery deletions that follow the review belong to the simplification pass's lane where they
  fall in its files; coordinate by the same content-versus-machinery boundary as before.

## What the saga needs back

The inventory as a PR against your own artifacts (or a single survey document in your feature
directory), labelled `saga:next-steps`, with the usual handoff. Published reviews wait for the
operator's direction per section 7a.
