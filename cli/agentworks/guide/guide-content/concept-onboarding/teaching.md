Progressive onboarding starts with discovery. Read kind and implementation topics, inspect existing
state, and prefer explicit verification commands when prediction is insufficient. A disabled or
not-ready item is information, not permission to repair it.

Proceed one step at a time. Explain what each command reads or changes, request consent at its
declared boundary, verify the observable result, and stop cleanly when consent is declined. A rerun
uses the same live facts, skips ready work, and reports disabled, not-ready, or unverifiable work.
