Resolve a named secret without another prompt when the operator's instruction and current envelope
already cover value-free proof for that exact name. The action's authorization class identifies the
boundary; consent comes from the operator's explicit instruction, not ritual reconfirmation. Ask
once for an uncovered name, sensitive content access, interactive authentication, or another
material expansion. A clear instruction covering that expansion is already the decision. Honor
refusal and per-action confirmation.

Do not display, log, retain, or interpolate a resolved value into a command or report. A preview,
readiness row, or guide rendering is prediction, not proof, and never authorization by itself.
Presence-only discovery does not authorize reading secret content.
